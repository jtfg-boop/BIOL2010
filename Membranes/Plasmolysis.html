Before you watch: plasmolysis is osmosis happening to a plant cell

1. The setup: a plant cell is a solution inside a semi-permeable membrane. A plant cell's cytoplasm and its large central vacuole contain many dissolved solutes, each wrapped in a hydration shell of water molecules. The plasma membrane is the semi-permeable membrane: water can cross it, but most solutes can't. Outside the membrane sits the cell wall, which is rigid but fully permeable, so water and solutes pass through it freely. The cell wall doesn't decide where water goes; it only limits how much the cell can swell or shrink in shape.
