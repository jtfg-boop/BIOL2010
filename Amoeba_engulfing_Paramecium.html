An amoeba is a single-celled organism (protist) that moves by extrusions of the cell membrane called pseudopods ("false feet"). Other cells, even animal white blood cells, can move in similar ways through amoeboid movement.

This video shows an amoeba engulfing a Paramecium via phagocytosis, trapping it in a food vacuole. Lysosomes then fuse with the vacuole and release digestive enzymes that kill and break down the prey.
