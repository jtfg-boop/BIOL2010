Volvox Volvox is a colonial green alga made of hundreds of cells forming a hollow sphere, and it's a classic example of cell specialization. Most cells are small, flagellated somatic cells that beat together to move the colony toward light — these cannot reproduce. A few larger cells called gonidia are specialized for reproduction, dividing to form new colonies. This split between movement/survival cells and reproductive cells is called germline-soma differentiation, marking a key evolutionary step toward true multicellularity.
