Equilibrium: A ⇌ B

Every dot is one molecule. Each step, reactant dots (gray) can convert to product (teal) and product dots can convert back. The equilibrium constant K is the product-to-reactant ratio the mixture settles at — it sets how the two rates compare, and therefore how lopsided the final mixture is. Watch the flashes: at equilibrium the flipping never stops; only the totals stop changing.
